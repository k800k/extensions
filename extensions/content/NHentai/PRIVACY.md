# Privacy assessment for NHentai

The extension sends search terms, gallery identifiers, pagination values, ordinary HTTP metadata, and service cookies to the three declared nHentai hosts through MangaReader's brokered client. Cookies may be created during a Cloudflare challenge handoff and remain under MangaReader's cookie controls.

The extension does not request a login, store account credentials, access favorites, transmit data to analytics services, or declare any third-party host. MangaReader and the remote service may retain network logs according to their own policies. Users can remove the extension and clear its brokered cookies using application controls.
